# _kupé_
_kupé_(_lithuanian_) - private train cabin or, now, a train travel planning app. We foster the idea of a sustainable and well connected Europe. Our app will ease the burden of planning, all is needed - desired destinations, we will do the rest. Hotel suggestions will be provided, train schedules (current or predicted based on previous years) will be shown, metro systems resolved, and the most popular attractions will be recommended, all worries resolved in a simple click. Travel plans can be saved, changed or shared - whatever your heart desires. Download _kupé_ now and enjoy the travel Europe has to offer.
